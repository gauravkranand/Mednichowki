Primary School
The primary school of Mednichowki also has a great history behind it. Firstly, the primary school used to be in the market, but due to some serious reason, it shifted at the back of the market in front of Durgasthan, near about in the year 1950. The first principal of this school was Dhaneshwari Devi. At present all the responsibility is under Lalita Kumari. The total number of student in this school is 250 .The school has a class up to 5th standard. The total number of teacher is 6.The school has a higher percentage of girl's student.

This school is ranked first in the particular block. The school also organizes cultural and Co-Curricular activities for creating interest among the children. The schools have a further plan to open hostel facility for poor & physically disabled student.
