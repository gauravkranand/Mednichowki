Panchayat Leaders In This Village
ZILA PARISHAD
 After the Grampanchayati raj act. came into existence, as per told by the people the first is not known but the  zila Parishad before the present was Manoj kumar who was on this position till 10 years. He was    from Manikpur. At present,the zila parishad is Amit sagar
AMIT SAGAR who is on this post from one and half years.his Grandfather name was Ram Chandra Prasad Mahto. He is Governing the area from Rasalpur to Manikpur under which six panchayat comes which are Tajpur,Vansipur,khaba rajpur,Kiranpur,Abgil rampur and Khawadpur panchayat. All the developement plans at the district level are taken up by him.He also take help of panchayat samitis in a particular panchayat.
At this time,as told by him developement in the field of education is going on and has also been done upto a certain extent. By seeing the increase percentage of developement graph in Mednichowki he told us that Mednichowki has capacity to turn themselves into a separate block.
He works on the panchayat one by one which means they work on a particular panchayat at a time and if it has been completed then they move to another one.
At present, the tree growing by Mukhiya and development in agriculture field by big farmer yojanas is in progress.
PRAMUKH
Suryaghadha Prakhand has 28 panchayat under it,one of them is Mednichowki.
As told by the people,after the rule of Grampanchayati raj act the first Pramukh of the area was Praveen Kumar who won this post in 2001. At this time,the present Pramukh is Ms Nita Devi
MS.NITA DEVI ,d/o-Nageshwar Prasad Singh. Her husband name is Jawahar Prasad Singh. The main work of Pramukh is to assign and look after the yojanas when they hand it over to the panchs. They give more focus on the yojanas related to education,sadak,bijli and krishi.
MUKHIYA
As per the record,the first Mukhiya after the Grampanchayati raj act was Ram Kishun Prasad Verma. His area was from kiranpur to amarpur.The name of the Grampanchayat was khawbha japani.He was continuously on this post for 39 years. Due to low BP and sugar he died in 1998 by a sudden heart attack.
After him,his son Vijay kumar verma became Mukhiya from 2001-2006.
Then after when ladies seat became reserved Vijay kumar verma wife Madhvi Lata
MADHVI LATA ,d/o-Hari Prasad Mahto became Mukhiya in 2006 and still present.... . The up-Mukhiya in the time of Ram kishun prasad verma from 1967 to 1998 was Jagdish Mahto.
Lalit Narayan singh also known as Laloo jee in the year1984 when he was a part of Telephone Advisory committee,he wrote application for electrical power and communication in this area. The telephone line came in 1998 by his hard labour.
SARPANCH
After India got its independence and panchayati raj act, came into existence the first Sarpanch as told by the older people of the village was Basudeo mahto,s/o sri badri mahto,village:-khawba,district:-lakhisharay,firstly known as mongyh. The grandson of late Basudeo Mahto is Banke Bihari Mahto,who is retired captain in Indian Army. The joining year of captain Banke Bihari is 1976 and retirement year is 31st january 2009. At present he is project manager in Megha developer pvt.Ltd in west bengal.Basudeo Mahto died in 1963. The second Sarpanch Vishnu Deo Mahto was born in 1943,who completed his education in amarpur high school. His father name was Lt.Sadhu Mahto,vill:-khawbha,po:-kiranpur,District:-lakhisharay. He was Sarpanch till 20 years,with wining two time election. He was also doing a private job under Lt.Jamuna Prasad Mahto,maternal grandson Lt.Sri Babu Mahto till 25 years. The wife name of vishnu deo mahto is Samudri Devi.He has 2 sons and 1 daughter. At this time,the present Sarpanch is Sushila Devi
SUSHILA DEVI who has 11 panch under her. She is on this post from 22 may 2011.Her husband name is Pro.Dinesh Prasad Verma (MALLB). The main motive of sarpanch is to give justice to people in a particular panchayat. They are also known as Gramkachhari. Every single term are utilised for the development. The sarpanch is giving justice to the people from independence. The Rasan card facility are also looked over by the sarpanch.
PANCHAYAT SAMITI
As per the record,the first Panchayat samiti after the Grampanchayat act,Sanju kumari d/o-sri Tilakdhari Mahto became the first Panchayat samiti from 2001-2005. She got maried in the year 1990.Her husband Lt.Niranjan Mahto has a great support in making those five year successful one. In her leadership she did a major work by constructing the pulia(small bridge),road inside the village and the drainage system for the water to pass. she also did the work,on the level of block and panchayat. After completing her election period,she is teacher of science from 2006 in college. She also has two daughters who are doing higher studies. Sanju devi husband father sri Chandra Dev Singh who is still alive with a age of more than seventy five. He had a major role in supporting the family members.He worked at railway kharkhana in Jamalpur.

The second Panchayat samiti after Sanju Devi was Kishori Razak.__we do not have a detailed history about this persons. At present,the Panchayat samiti is Manoj Das
Mr.MANOJ DAS from may 2011.He was born on 1st november in 1971. His father name was Lt.Ram Kishun Das.He completed his education in munger and +2 studies also in munger from j.m.s college. He has 4 childrens.Beside this he also has a business of footwear and shoes. His work is to progress the yojanas made by the Government. Under his leadership,fourteenth finance aayog is yet to be progressed under which the investment is to be made in handpump,solar light,drainage system,electricity and many others.
