Places
There are different places in Medni chowki and every places has a special feature behind it. It is very exciting for anyone to visit each place one by one. The exciting feature of each and every place are described in brief.

The Police station which has an exciting history i.e. it was built when several policemen were killed by the criminal .

The Mosque shifted beside the road or we can say in the market in 1946 from bank of river ganga.

The Madrasa was opened 35-40 years ago as it was a golden opportunity for the poor muslim children to read.

The construction of Aanganbari kendra in 2004 now gave the right to poor children to read and enjoy the facility provided by the government.

The construction of Temple chandisthan at the same place where the pandit used to worship god. The construction of another Temple Durgasthan after the incident happened to natho saw by the people of kiranpur village.

The Primary School which is the no.1 ranked school in the particular block.

TheGramin Bank is present in the middle of the market.
