Aanganbari kendra
The government opened Aanganbari kendra in 2004 to teach the poor children of each and every society by bringing them into the kendra to study. At present the total number of students studying in the kendra is 40. The sevika Mita kumari manages and control over all the children. The head of the Aanganbari kendra(supervisor) is Shikha Devi. The kendra has a cook(sahayika), Manju kumari who cooks food for all the children.
